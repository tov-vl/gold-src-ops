# GoldSrcOps v2.4 Synthetic Monitoring Provider Decision

Decision date: 2026-09-04. Status: Grafana Cloud Synthetic Monitoring is the
preferred provider for a non-authoritative shadow rollout. Account eligibility,
raw-result export, independent retention, and failure classification must still
pass before `API-01` can leave `Draft`.

## Decision Scope

This decision compares managed external monitoring services against the
[provider-independent availability contract](v2.4-external-availability-monitoring.md).
It chooses the next implementation candidate; it does not create an account,
configure a production monitor, start an official SLI window, or claim that the
draft SLO is met.

Capabilities and prices were checked against vendor documentation on
2026-09-04. They must be rechecked before a paid plan is purchased or the
official measurement window is activated.

## Hard Gates

The selected path must provide:

- one canonical HTTPS execution every minute from exactly one external
  location;
- a 10-second complete timeout, no redirect following, normal certificate and
  hostname validation, and exact HTTP `200` validation;
- timestamped success and failure evidence that can be normalized without
  treating missing samples as success;
- an exportable monitor revision and a stable deduplication identity;
- at least 45 days of recoverable owner-controlled evidence;
- testable alert routing without changing the SLI result;
- an account and payment model the owner can use with accurate legal and
  residency information;
- no agent or private telemetry endpoint on either GoldSrcOps host.

## Candidate Comparison

| Candidate | Schedule and request controls | Result and revision access | Retention boundary | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grafana Cloud Synthetic Monitoring | HTTP checks support 10-3600-second frequency, 1-60-second timeout, status validation, redirect control, and certificate validation. | Per-check Prometheus metrics and logs, `config_version`, a Metrics query API, and UI/API/Terraform configuration are available. | Free telemetry retention is 14 days. Pro retains metrics for 13 months but logs for 30 days. Neither plan alone proves the required 45-day raw archive. | Preferred conditionally; prove export and archive during shadow validation. |
| Checkly | The free Hobby plan is limited to a two-minute maximum frequency; paid plans support shorter intervals. | Raw check runs include timestamps, outcome, location, and diagnostics. | Published raw retention is 7 days on Hobby and Starter, 30 days on Team, and 180 days on Enterprise. | Not selected; the self-service retention and cost shape do not fit this small workload. |
| Better Stack Uptime | Its monitor API exposes sub-minute frequency, timeout, expected status, TLS, and redirect options. | The response-time API returns regional timestamped samples. A complete canonical failure export was not established from the published API. | The documented response-time endpoint returns only the last 24 hours, requiring a tightly coupled export job. | Not selected; evidence completeness and recovery margin are weaker than Grafana Cloud. |
| UptimeRobot | Paid plans offer one-minute checks. | Availability history and API access are available. | During extended downtime the service progressively spaces out checks beyond the configured interval. | Rejected for the primary SLI because it does not preserve the fixed one-result-per-minute population. |

The comparison is intentionally narrow. Browser checks, private probes, status
pages, and bundled incident-management features do not improve the `API-01`
measurement and therefore do not compensate for a failed hard gate.

## Selected Path

Use Grafana Cloud Synthetic Monitoring for the 24-hour shadow rollout, subject
to all of the following conditions:

1. The owner can create and operate the account using accurate current details
   and the intended free or paid plan.
2. A saved HTTP check can enforce the complete probe contract and expose its
   effective configuration for review.
3. Prometheus samples and, where needed, probe logs can be exported with enough
   information to classify the canonical attempt and distinguish missing data.
4. Duplicate exports produce the same stable execution identity, while separate
   attempts do not collide.
5. Normalized records are copied to a private owner-controlled store outside
   the control-plane failure domain and can be recovered after at least 45 days.
6. A complete 24-hour shadow produces exactly 1,440 expected primary slots and
   passes every verification row in the availability contract.

Failure of any condition keeps `API-01` in `Draft`. It triggers a focused trial
of the next candidate or an independently hosted black-box probe; it does not
relax missing-data, TLS, schedule, or retention semantics.

## Shadow Topology

The initial Grafana Cloud configuration should contain three HTTP checks:

| Role | Target | Location | Frequency | SLI effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary readiness | `/health/ready` | Frankfurt | 60 seconds | Canonical `API-01` result |
| Diagnostic liveness | `/health/live` | Frankfurt | 60 seconds | Diagnosis only |
| Diagnostic readiness | `/health/ready` | Stockholm | 300 seconds | Regional diagnosis only |

The locations are public AWS probes and therefore do not share either
GoldSrcOps hosting provider. The shadow must confirm that both remain available
to the selected account before they are recorded as part of a monitor revision.
Changing the primary location later requires a reviewed revision and continuity
decision.

Each check uses `GET`, a 10-second timeout, redirect following disabled,
certificate validation enabled, exact status `200`, no request body or
credentials, and a stable project-specific user agent. Custom labels identify
the environment, role, and reviewed monitor revision without containing owner,
account, host, or provider identifiers.

## Execution Budget

Grafana bills one API test execution per probe location per started minute of
test duration. With each HTTP check bounded to 10 seconds, the proposed 30-day
usage is:

```text
primary readiness:       1 probe * 1 check * 43,200 / 1 = 43,200
diagnostic liveness:     1 probe * 1 check * 43,200 / 1 = 43,200
diagnostic readiness:    1 probe * 1 check * 43,200 / 5 =  8,640
total:                                                     95,040
free monthly allowance:                                  100,000
remaining margin:                                           4,960
```

The margin is deliberately small. Do not add a probe, shorten an interval, or
add another API check without recalculating the complete monthly execution and
telemetry budget. The UI usage calculator must be checked before saving each
monitor.

## Canonical Evidence And Retention

Grafana's displayed uptime is diagnostic, not the `API-01` calculation. Its
published query is based on reported samples and uses `max_over_time`, so it
does not implement the GoldSrcOps expected-slot denominator or missing-sample
policy.

The rollout must instead:

1. Query the primary `probe_success` series and supporting HTTP, DNS, TLS, and
   duration signals through the Prometheus HTTP API with an access-policy token
   limited to `metrics:read`.
2. Use probe logs only where they are required to classify a failed execution;
   never retain response bodies, headers, cookies, or credentials.
3. Normalize results against generated UTC minute slots and wait the contractual
   five-minute ingestion grace period before recording `missing`.
4. Export with an overlap window and deterministic deduplication so a transient
   exporter failure can catch up while source telemetry is still retained.
5. Write append-only normalized records to a private Backblaze B2 bucket or
   isolated prefix with a dedicated restricted application key. Do not reuse
   the PostgreSQL restic repository namespace or credentials.
6. Retain normalized records, configuration revisions, evaluator revisions, and
   review manifests for at least 45 days; prefer 90 days.
7. Rehearse download and deterministic re-evaluation before activation.

The export scheduler must run outside the production control-plane runtime.
GitHub Actions may be evaluated for this catch-up-capable export job because it
does not define probe timing; it remains unsuitable as the canonical monitor.
Placement and credentials are decided in the rollout slice.

## Configuration And Credential Boundaries

- Export and review a sanitized check configuration. Provider resource IDs and
  account metadata stay outside Git.
- Treat the Synthetic Monitoring configuration token as high privilege: vendor
  documentation states that it has no expiration and no resource-scoped RBAC.
  Create it only when needed, store it outside the repository, and revoke or
  rotate it after setup unless automated provisioning requires it.
- Use a separate least-privilege `metrics:read` access-policy token for the
  evidence exporter.
- Use a separate bucket-scoped object-storage key for normalized evidence.
- Keep alert-route credentials separate from monitor and archive credentials.
- Do not expose any of these credentials to the public dashboard planned later
  in v2.4.

## Alert Boundary

Create a custom Grafana alert for the primary check rather than accepting a
vendor aggregate as the SLI. The target policy remains three consecutive bad or
missing primary slots to notify and two consecutive good slots to resolve.
Notification suppression, retries, and diagnostic probes do not alter stored
canonical outcomes or error-budget consumption.

The shadow rollout must exercise the notification route against a disposable or
otherwise safe target. It must not deliberately break the production API,
certificate, DNS, or database.

## Exit Conditions

Replace Grafana Cloud as the primary measurement source if:

- truthful account operation is unavailable;
- the effective check follows redirects or weakens certificate validation;
- exported metrics and logs cannot identify every canonical minute and failure
  outcome without relying on dashboard aggregates;
- export gaps cannot be recovered within source retention;
- the independent archive or restore rehearsal is unreliable;
- required usage exceeds the approved budget; or
- a provider change makes fixed one-minute execution nondeterministic.

The provider-independent schema, expected-slot evaluator, and external archive
are the exit path. Historical records remain tied to their immutable monitor and
evaluator revisions; they are never rewritten to resemble a replacement
provider's native format.

## Next Slice

The measurement rollout should proceed in this order:

1. Validate account availability and initialize a Grafana Cloud stack without
   purchasing a paid plan.
2. Configure the three disabled or non-authoritative shadow checks and export a
   sanitized effective configuration.
3. Implement and test the normalized exporter, independent B2 retention, and
   deterministic evaluator fixtures.
4. Enable the shadow checks, verify alert routing and safe failure fixtures, and
   collect 24 complete hours.
5. Review all 1,440 expected primary slots. Only then record the activation
   tuple and start a new prospective `API-01` window.
