## Boundary

Addon 0.38.0 extends the private RU/EN `/rules` guide with the current automatic
respawn delay, spawn protection duration and early-removal conditions. It reads
`mp_forcerespawn`, `mp_respawn_immunitytime` and
`mp_respawn_immunity_force_unset` on every admitted request. No gameplay CVAR,
spawn hook, combat behavior, storage format, map addon or application changes.

The existing five-second shared alias cooldown, effective-language projection,
dead-player/spectator access and invalid/bot/HLTV/disabled exclusions remain.
Viewing rules does not load identity, flush preferences, write vaults, refresh
leaderboards or change health/inventory. Existing weapon/reward/rank paragraphs
remain complete inside the previous 1536-cell MOTD buffer.

## Display contract

| Setting | Display |
| --- | --- |
| Valid finite duration in `[-3600, 0]` | Feature explicitly disabled |
| Duration in `(0, 0.01)` | HTML-escaped `less than 0.01` seconds |
| Duration in `[0.01, 3600]` | Seconds rounded to two decimal places |
| Missing pointer, non-finite or out-of-range duration | Settings unavailable |
| Protection removal mode `0` | Actions do not remove protection early |
| Mode `1` | Active actions, including movement or attack, remove protection |
| Mode `2` | Primary or secondary attack removes protection |
| Missing or other mode, including fractions | Early-removal settings unavailable |

Removal text appears only when protection is enabled. The guide reports engine
settings, not a personal countdown or a guarantee of elapsed time from a kill:
the engine also owns death animation, team/joining state and scheduling.
Timing display uses rounded integer hundredths because the pinned native float
formatter produced `2.49` for `2.50` in the isolated engine. Unsupported settings
are not clamped into a different claim, and reading never writes a CVAR.

Semantics were checked against the pinned
[ReGameDLL 5.30.0.814 configuration](https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/blob/5.30.0.814/dist/game.cfg)
and [player implementation](https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/blob/5.30.0.814/regamedll/dlls/player.cpp).
Other plugins overriding these engine mechanics are outside this guide's claim.

and check the complete window: respawn delay `1.00` seconds, protection up to
`2.00` seconds, primary/secondary attack removal, and all previous paragraphs.
Close with OK and reopen after five seconds through another existing alias.
Repeat on EN. Check readable text, intact UTF-8, complete captions and normal
return of controls.

Required evidence is two complete `/rules` screenshots, RU and EN, plus the
owner's checklist result, finish time in MSK and whether video settings changed.
Disabled/unsupported variants remain isolated-engine evidence. This does not
replace PvP verification of actual protection behavior or human activity data.
