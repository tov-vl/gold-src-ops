# GoldSrcOps v2.4 Synthetic Monitoring Rollout

Rollout date: 2026-09-04. Status: three non-authoritative Grafana Cloud
Synthetic Monitoring checks are enabled under monitor revision
`v2-4-shadow-001`. `API-01` remains `Draft`; these samples do not enter an
official SLI window.

## Account Boundary

The owner created the Grafana Cloud account with accurate current details and
initialized Synthetic Monitoring without adding a payment card or purchasing a
paid plan. The account currently has the standard 14-day trial. Grafana's
published billing documentation states that an account moves to the Free plan
when the trial completes, and current pricing describes that plan as free with
no credit card required.

Successful signup proves current technical account availability only. Continued
service remains subject to the provider's terms, export controls, product
changes, and free-plan limits. GoldSrcOps therefore keeps its normalized schema
and independent archive as an exit path.

Provider account metadata, resource identifiers, owner identity, and
credentials are intentionally excluded from this document and from Git.

## Sanitized Effective Configuration

| Job | Target | Role | Public probe | Frequency | Estimated executions per 31 days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| `goldsrcops-api-ready-primary-shadow` | `https://api.goldsrcops.com/health/ready` | `primary` | Frankfurt, DE (AWS) | 60 seconds | 44,640 |
| `goldsrcops-api-live-diagnostic-shadow` | `https://api.goldsrcops.com/health/live` | `diagnostic` | Frankfurt, DE (AWS) | 60 seconds | 44,640 |
| `goldsrcops-api-ready-diagnostic-shadow` | `https://api.goldsrcops.com/health/ready` | `diagnostic` | Stockholm, SE (AWS) | 300 seconds | 8,928 |

Every check has the following effective request contract:

- HTTP `GET` with no body, authentication, cookies, query parameters, or
  response-body assertion;
- a 10-second complete timeout;
- redirect following disabled;
- target certificate and hostname validation enabled, with TLS required;
- exactly HTTP `200` accepted;
- `User-Agent: GoldSrcOps-Availability/1.0`;
- the default reduced metric set; and
- built-in per-check alerts disabled.

The custom labels are:

| Label | Primary value | Diagnostic value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `environment` | `production` | `production` |
| `role` | `primary` | `diagnostic` |
| `monitor_revision` | `v2-4-shadow-001` | `v2-4-shadow-001` |

The job names and labels explicitly identify this revision as shadow evidence.
They do not create an activation timestamp or make Grafana's displayed uptime
canonical.

## Initial Verification

The provider UI confirmed that both selected public probes were online and
available to the account. Before each check was saved, its interactive test
completed target resolution and an HTTP request-response round trip without a
reported error. The saved forms were then re-read and matched the sanitized
configuration above. Initial scheduled results from both Frankfurt checks and
the Stockholm check reported 100% uptime and reachability. These early provider
aggregates validate setup only; they are not an `API-01` result.

Grafana's per-check calculator reports a combined maximum-month estimate of
98,208 API test executions:

```text
primary readiness:       1 probe * 1 check * 44,640 / 1 = 44,640
diagnostic liveness:     1 probe * 1 check * 44,640 / 1 = 44,640
diagnostic readiness:    1 probe * 1 check * 44,640 / 5 =  8,928
total:                                                     98,208
free monthly allowance:                                  100,000
remaining margin:                                           1,792
```

This uses a conservative 31-day month. Do not add saved checks, locations, or a
shorter interval without reviewing the complete account usage estimate. Manual
tests and temporary failure-path checks must also stay within the remaining
allowance.

## Remaining Gates

The checks may continue producing shadow telemetry, but `API-01` cannot leave
`Draft` until all of the following are complete:

1. **Live export proved 2026-09-04:** the dedicated `metrics:read` credential
   produced one bounded normalized export without retaining response content;
   provider failure-detail data still must prove transport classification;
2. **Implemented 2026-09-04:** deterministic normalization, bounded overlap
   export, source-timestamp correlation, create-only JSONL, deduplication, and
   expected-slot evaluation;
3. **Live archive proved 2026-09-04:** a separate private B2 namespace with split
   prefix-scoped reader/writer credentials accepted one content-addressed upload,
   rejected a sequential duplicate before a second upload, and returned the same
   25-record segment for SHA-256 and deterministic report verification;
4. **Core fixtures implemented 2026-09-04:** missing-slot, retry, DNS, TLS,
   timeout, redirect, non-`200`, 1,440-slot, and 216/217 budget behavior; a
   provider failure-detail adapter still must prove the no-status transport
   categories without disrupting the production endpoint;
5. test the custom notification route and resolution policy; and
6. complete and review a contiguous 24-hour shadow window before recording a
   prospective activation tuple.

No user-managed Synthetic Monitoring configuration token was created for the
manual setup. The Metrics API and B2 credentials are separate, narrowly scoped,
time-bounded where supported, and stored outside Git. The next operational slice
implements a serialized GitHub-hosted scheduler outside the production
control-plane runtime. Its environment secrets and pinned exporter revision are
not configured yet, and its repository activation variable remains disabled,
so no scheduled archive evidence is claimed. The local and scheduled workflows
are documented in
`docs/v2.4-availability-evidence-exporter.md` and
`ops/availability/README.md`.
