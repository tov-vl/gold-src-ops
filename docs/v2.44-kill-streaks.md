# v2.44 Kill Streaks

Status as of 2026-10-05: focused engine, translation and immutable package
checks passed locally. Product CI and addon installation are pending.

## Behavior

Weapon addon 0.14.0 tracks consecutive legitimate enemy-human kills while
the attacker is alive. Its existing scoring exclusions still apply: no bot,
HLTV, teamkill, suicide, world, disconnected or non-playing kill credit.
Posthumous kills cannot begin or extend a streak, although the existing
kill-statistics rules are unchanged.

Every death resets the victim's current streak, including deaths excluded
from scoring and deaths while the addon is disabled. The best streak survives
death but both counters reset on disconnect, slot reuse and map reload. This
is connection/map-local feedback, not a persistent record or global ranking.

The compact RU/EN HUD gains one current-streak line. `/stats` adds a private
current/best line under its existing cooldown and retains its saved-total or
map-total response. Personal RU/EN messages occur exactly at 3, 5 and 10 kills
per life; a new life can earn those milestones again. No public broadcast,
sound, damage bonus, new preference, storage record or rank reward is added.
Existing HUD toggle/menu priority, kill healing and ammo refill are unchanged.
`goldsrcops_weapons_status` exposes the bounded `KILL_STREAK_STATUS` policy.

schema-2 packaging passed. The short isolated engine run covers native deaths,

rendering and multiplayer pacing remain optional, untested MVP observations.
