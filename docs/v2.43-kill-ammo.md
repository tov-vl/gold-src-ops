# v2.43 Kill Ammo Refill

Status as of 2026-10-05: product PR merged after required CI; weapon addon
0.13.0 installed and independently accepted. No tag or control-plane release.

## Behavior

Weapon addon 0.13.0 tops up the reserve ammunition of an alive human player
after a legitimate enemy-human kill. It uses the same eligibility as scoring
and kill healing. Suicide, teamkill, world death, bot/HLTV encounters and
disconnected/non-playing participants do not earn a refill. Posthumous kills
cannot refill ammunition.

Only owned loadout weapons are eligible: MP5 (120 reserve), AK-47/M4A1 (90),
USP (100), Glock (120), Desert Eagle (35). Existing greater reserves are kept.
The shared MP5/Glock 9mm pool is filled once to the common limit. Changing
next-spawn weapon choices cannot grant a weapon or refill an unowned pool.
Clip ammunition, active weapon and reload behavior remain unchanged.

Server cvar `goldsrcops_kill_ammo` defaults to `1`; `0` disables the refill
independently of scoring and healing. Global addon disable also suppresses it.
`goldsrcops_weapons_status` reports `KILL_AMMO_STATUS`. There is no new player
preference, translation, storage record or rank reward.
