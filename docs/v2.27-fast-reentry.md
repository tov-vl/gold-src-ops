# v2.27 Fast Re-entry Product Slice

Status: local solo mechanic check and fixed profile artifact on 2026-09-24;
the profile-switch workflow, host transition, and release have not been
performed.

## Product Hypothesis

Let a player return to play shortly after death instead of waiting for the
next classic round. Start with a fixed, team-based `fast-reentry-v1` experiment,
not a claim that a complete Team Deathmatch mode or player progression exists.
The accepted `public-classic-v1` remains the live and rollback profile until a
separately reviewed game-host transition passes its gates.

The first behavioral target is respawn within three seconds of a controlled
death, with at most two seconds of visible protection that ends on attack.
Exact cvar values must be frozen after local runtime testing, not inferred
from configuration documentation alone. Keep the existing five-map inventory;
test one map first and verify profile reapplication after a map change before
considering the rotation accepted.

## Native First

The pinned ReGameDLL_CS `5.30.0.814` documents `mp_forcerespawn`,
`mp_respawn_immunitytime`, and `mp_respawn_immunity_force_unset`. It also
documents `mp_round_infinite` and `mp_roundrespawn_time`, whose interaction
with team elimination and objective maps needs an executable local check.
The first increment should use only reviewed native settings. Do not enable
random spawns: this version requires map `.nav` files, and spawn safety has
not been established for the accepted rotation.

The [AlliedModders ReDeathmatch thread](https://forums.alliedmods.net/showthread.php?t=346875)
is useful for later weapon-menu and spawn-placement research, but its
[upstream repository](https://github.com/ReDeathmatch/ReDeathmatch_AMXX) was
archived in 2025. Do not download, bundle, or activate it for this slice.
Revisit a maintained plugin or a small owned extension only if the native
experiment exposes a concrete player-facing gap.

Source for the pinned native settings:
[ReGameDLL_CS 5.30.0.814 game.cfg](https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/blob/5.30.0.814/dist/game.cfg).
Player-filter semantics:
[AMX Mod X `get_players` reference](https://www.amxmodx.org/api/amxmodx/get_players).

## Existing Runtime Boundaries

- `ops/gameserver/managed-profile.sh` fixes `public-classic-v1` and reapplies
  it through `mapchangecfgfile`. A new profile must not overwrite its active
  configuration or silently change its marker and rollback inputs.
- `ops/gameserver/guarded-autostart.sh` verifies the active profile's exact
  hash and policy marker. `ops/gameserver/game-event-persistent.sh` accepts
  the classic profile for activation and binds active game and agent files to
  reviewed hashes. Its persistent game guard does not currently bind the
  active public configuration, profile, mapcycle, or profile marker. There is
  no approved in-place profile-switch operation while persistent telemetry is
  active. Refuse a switch until that gap is closed and the exact rollback
  boundary can be proved.
- The v2.26 producer emits anonymous `round.ended` records when a round ends
  with at least one non-HLTV client. Its `get_players(..., "h")` guard does not
  exclude bots; the accepted host currently has zero bots. Suppressing or
  extending round endings can reduce these records without indicating
  delivery failure. Do not use round-event count as the fast-reentry
  acceptance signal, introduce player identity, or silently redefine the
  existing event contract. Fix and test bot-only suppression before any
  separate decision to enable bots.
- No API, Web, PostgreSQL, Auth0, alert delivery, firewall, or public endpoint
  change belongs to this slice.

## Local Feasibility Check

On 2026-09-24, Docker Desktop became available. The existing local
`goldsrcops-rehlds-smoke` image contains ReHLDS `3.14.0.857` and ReGameDLL_CS
`5.28.0.756`, so its bundled binaries cannot establish behavior for the
accepted host. The pinned ReHLDS `3.15.0.896` and ReGameDLL_CS `5.30.0.814`
release ZIPs matched the SHA-256 values in `ops/gameserver/runtime-install.sh`;
both detached signatures verified against its embedded ReHLDS Team key and
fingerprint. A disposable container reused the older image's HLDS/game assets
with only those two pinned binaries overlaid; it is not an exact replica of
the accepted host. ReGameDLL printed its `5.30.0.814` version, Steam
connected, and a loopback-only A2S probe answered on `de_dust2`. The game
host was untouched.

At startup, `game.cfg` replaced the experimental command-line values with
its defaults (`mp_forcerespawn 0`, `mp_roundrespawn_time 20`, immunity time
`0`, force-unset mode `1`). Setting the candidate values in the live local
console and querying again returned `1`, `-1`, `2`, and `2` respectively.
The player reported nearly immediate respawn after `kill`, a visible short
protection effect, and that effect ending on attack; A2S returned zero players
and bots after exit. This is a qualitative solo observation, not a measured
three-second bound or proof of damage immunity. Map-change persistence and
objective-round behavior were not verified. The fixed profile must execute
after `game.cfg` and reapply through the reviewed map-change hook.

The upstream `game.cfg` makes `mp_forcerespawn 1`,
`mp_roundrespawn_time -1`, `mp_respawn_immunitytime 2`,
`mp_respawn_immunity_effects 1`, and
`mp_respawn_immunity_force_unset 2` the locally exercised candidate, not a
host-accepted profile. In particular, mode `2` removes protection on attack
rather than movement. Keep `mp_round_infinite` undecided until a client test
checks both team elimination and objective-map endings. Do not add these
settings to the managed host configuration from source inspection alone.

## Fixed Profile And Guard Contract

`ops/gameserver/fast-reentry-v1.cfg` freezes the five locally exercised
native settings alongside the accepted classic rule values and five-map
rotation. It reapplies through its own `mapchangecfgfile` hook. The public
configuration and accepted classic profile are unchanged. This file is not
installed on the game host, and the local solo observation does not prove
objective-round behavior or map-change reapplication.

`game-event-persistent.sh` continues to accept the deployed schema-1 policy
marker. A future profile-switch workflow may create schema 2 only for
`fast-reentry-v1`: it binds the public configuration, runtime-enabled marker,
active profile marker, fixed profile, and mapcycle to exact hashes. The game
guard rejects drift in any of those files and requires the selected profile
to be loaded exactly once with a map-change hook. The existing destructive
persistent rollback refuses schema 2; switching back to classic must be a
separate operation that preserves queue, spool, and identity.

The focused `tools/smoke/gameserver-fast-reentry.sh` covers the fixed file,
schema-1 compatibility, schema-2 drift rejection, conflicting profile loads,
and rollback refusal. It does not exercise a live host, service ordering,
backup restoration, external gate, or interrupted switch. Those checks still
belong to the unimplemented plan-first transition workflow.

## Proposed Profile Transition

This design requires a dedicated plan-first workflow and focused fault-injection
smoke before any host apply. It is not an instruction to run the existing
`managed-profile.sh`, `guarded-autostart.sh --disable`, or
`game-event-persistent.sh --rollback` as a mode switch. The last of these stops
both services, seals queue and spool into an owner-only evidence record, and
restores a plugin-free baseline; it does not preserve an online telemetry
session for immediate profile toggling.

1. Preflight the exact accepted `public-classic-v1` files, both persistent
   guards, rollback inputs, service invocation state, and empty queue/spool
   aggregates. Refuse drift, an unsettled aggregate, concurrent transition,
   or connected players. Serialize with both existing transition locks using
   a reviewed lock order or a single shared lock contract.
2. Stage root-only exact-byte backups of the public configuration, profile,
   mapcycle, active-profile marker, persistent policy marker, and installed
   guard. Record hashes and file metadata. Keep the agent identity and its
   queue/spool in place; neither copy secrets into the bundle nor reset state.
3. Stop the game to close producer intake, wait for the agent to settle, then
   stop the agent. Disable both boot entries before changing files so a reboot
   during the transition cannot start a half-installed policy. Do not couple
   the two systemd units.
4. Install a distinct fixed profile and map-change hook, rebind the public
   configuration and profile marker, and extend the persistent game guard to
   verify the selected profile ID, exact public/profile/mapcycle hashes, and
   hook. Stage the new guard and its hash-bound policy marker while both units
   are stopped. The agent guard and identity remain independently enforced.
5. Start game and agent separately. Require fresh invocation IDs, current
   guard passes, zero unexpected restarts, external A2S, read-only RCON cvars,
   and unchanged aggregate queue/spool state. Only after these gates pass may
   both independent boot entries be enabled. Bound the external gate by a
   timeout; no unattended retry.
6. On any failure, stop and disable both units, restore the exact classic
   profile and guard bytes, and start each service against its restored guard.
   Preserve any new queue/spool data rather than replacing it with a stale
   snapshot. If restoration or evidence preservation cannot be proven, leave
   the units disabled and retain the owner-only recovery record. Never remove
   the identity or silently invoke the destructive pilot rollback.

The local smoke must cover the non-mutating plan, both locks, exact backups,
guard rejection on each profile-file drift, service ordering, map-change hook,
interrupt and timeout rollback, and queue/spool preservation. A live R3 switch
is a later decision after the playable local experiment and that smoke pass.

## Implementation And Acceptance Gates

1. In a disposable local game runtime, confirm the pinned cvar behavior with
   one human client: controlled death, bounded respawn, protection visuals
   ending on attack, and stable team membership. This cannot prove damage
   immunity against another player. Check objective-map round behavior before
   freezing `mp_round_infinite` or related settings.
2. Add a fixed, plan-first profile and focused smoke for rendering, valid
   values, map-change reapplication, exact file ownership and hashes, refusal
   on drift, and restoration of the accepted classic profile. Define the
   transition around the active persistent guard and retained queue/spool,
   without weakening either service's boot checks.
3. Treat a live game-host switch as R3 and a separate decision. Rehearse
   rollback first; require external A2S, read-only RCON values, active guard
   checks, and unchanged agent aggregate state. With one consenting player,
   verify join, controlled death/respawn, map change, and return to classic.
   Stop on an unexplained gate failure without automatic retry.
4. A solo session does not prove spawn fairness, protection against damage
   from another player, team balance, or sustained play. Keep the mode
   experimental until a later multi-player session can test those claims.
   Ranks, weapon menus, custom spawn points, bots, and third-party plugins
   are separate slices.

The next gate is the plan-first transition and its fault-injection smoke,
followed by a local map-change/objective-round check. This document does not
authorize a host change or assert that the proposed gameplay works in
production.
