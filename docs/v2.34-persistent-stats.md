# v2.34 Persistent Player Stats

Status as of 2026-10-04: addon 0.5.0 is installed on the pilot. Focused storage
lifecycle/regression checks, required PR CI and independent external/host
postflight passed. The owner also confirmed single-client `/stats` display,
death increment and reconnect restoration. Multiplayer scoring and abrupt
disk-failure durability remain untested MVP limitations.

## Behavior

`/stats` (team chat and console forms included) shows saved kills, deaths and
K/D for a valid engine-supplied SteamID. The existing human/enemy scoring and
two-second command cooldown remain unchanged. `/top` still shows at most five
connected players' current connection/map scores; it is not a lifetime table.
Weapon selection, first-spawn menu, map addon and telemetry are unchanged.

The stock pinned nVault module stores only a canonical SteamID key and
`1 <kills> <deaths>` in `goldsrcops-player-stats-v1`. Public-universe SteamID
renderings 0/1 normalize to one key. BOT, HLTV, LAN, pending, zero/shared and
malformed IDs are rejected. Nickname and IP are never fallback identities.
Authorization can precede or follow entry; scoring before a valid identity is
not retroactively imported. A concurrent duplicate identity cannot overwrite
the first connection's totals. Names, IPs and player records are not logged,
exported, sent to the control plane or included in operator status output.

Each accepted death saves the affected totals immediately, with nVault
readback. Joining without scoring does not create/overwrite a zero record.
Reconnect and normal map/plugin/process shutdown retain totals; local map
scores reset. Counters saturate at one billion. An unavailable vault or invalid
record falls back to clearly labelled unsaved connection/map stats. Invalid
records are retained, not reset or silently migrated. There is no automatic
prune/reset, schema migration, global leaderboard, rank or acquisition of a
second player's identity. Persistent totals begin with this version; historical
connection-only scores cannot be reconstructed.

Use the pinned [nVault API](https://www.amxmodx.org/api/nvault/nvault_open) and
the [authorization lifecycle](https://www.amxmodx.org/api/amxmodx/client_authorized).
Readback proves the nVault operation, not filesystem fsync or crash-proof
durability. Sudden power/disk failure and cross-host disaster recovery are not
claimed. The owner should include the closed vault/journal in a separate
private game-data backup before relying on this for important progression.
