## Anomalies And Limits

The initial format check rejected CRLF in authored files. Only current-slice
paths were normalized to LF, then format/build/test/audit passed. A read-only
operator preflight initially required every service to expose a Docker
healthcheck; inspect confirmed API/Caddy have none. The existing rollout rule
(running and healthy where a check exists) accepted the fresh baseline.
Neither diagnostic pass changed production. The initial product CI was
superseded by the complete-header assertion refinement before tagging; the
final exact-head run passed. Rollback was not required.

Refreshing SSR does not trigger A2S polling or promise immediate server changes.
Transport failures retain a known public connection command, but a returned
unavailable SSR projection removes controls until recovery. Without JavaScript,
status/age remain those of SSR until normal reload. An older open page after
Web rollback can reject the earlier markup and show unknown until reload.
Existing in-memory BFF sessions retain the Web restart boundary; authenticated
continuity is not claimed.

No production backup, restore, migration, interactive login, Steam launch,
gameplay-client session or personal notification delivery was performed. First
weekly backup on 2026-10-11 at 04:00 MSK, empty server only; deferred v2.55 client
acceptance and two-player play remain independent.

See [contract](v2.60-play-status-refresh.md), [notes](release-notes-v2.60.md)
and [decision](architecture-decisions.md#decision-43-refresh-public-connection-status-without-browser-api-access).
The final evidence PR is D0 and requires no rollout or repeated full suite.
