# v2.46 HUD Continuity And Menu Coexistence

Status as of 2026-10-08: addon 0.16.0 merged, installed once and independently
verified on the active game server. Required and post-merge CI passed.
The owner completed client testing and accepted the current HUD as-is.

## Owner Feedback And Change

After testing v2.45, the owner confirmed the functional checks were correct but
reported periodic HUD disappearance and reappearance. The owner also requested
moving the HUD higher and keeping it visible while `/menu` is open.

The previous Director frame lifetime and refresh interval were both exactly
one second. This leaves no margin when a timer tick or network delivery is late
and is consistent with the reported flicker. Addon 0.16.0 keeps the one-second
refresh and extends each frame to 1.5 seconds without fades or display effects.
This creates a half-second delivery margin; it does not guarantee continuity
during a longer stall or prove the client's rendering behavior.

Statistics/streak now start at `(0.02, 0.14)` and rank at `(0.02, 0.21)`, moving
both blocks upward by 8% of screen height, or 128 pixels at 2560x1600. The font,
two-message split and translated text stay the same. Each message remains below
the Director limit. Menu visibility no longer suppresses HUD updates, and opening
the hub, settings or weapons does not clear its refresh state. The menu itself
remains active while the HUD refreshes.

The HUD toggle, addon disable, death, spectator and bot exclusions still stop
refresh. A previously sent frame expires within 1.5 seconds. No individually
addressable Director channel exists, so the addon relies on bounded expiry.
Player statistics/preferences, scoring, ranks, weapons and dictionaries keep
their existing contracts. No storage migration or client-file change is needed.
