Status as of 2026-10-05: focused local checks passed; required CI and
addon-only target installation remain pending. No tag or control-plane release.

## Scope

Weapon addon 0.12.0 restores 15 HP to an alive human player after a legitimate
enemy-human kill, capped at 100 HP. It shares the existing scoring eligibility:
suicide, teamkill, world death, bots, HLTV, disconnected and non-playing
participants do not heal. A posthumous kill can still score but cannot revive
or heal its attacker. Existing externally boosted health above 100 is retained.

Server cvar `goldsrcops_kill_heal` defaults to `15`; `0` disables healing
without disabling scoring. Values are bounded to 0-100 before integer
conversion; fractional amounts round down. Global addon disable also disables
healing. No weapon, armor, menu, translation, rank, scoring or storage-schema
change. The existing native health display reflects the resulting HP.
`goldsrcops_weapons_status` exposes a bounded `KILL_HEAL_STATUS` policy line.
