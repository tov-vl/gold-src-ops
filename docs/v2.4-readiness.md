# GoldSrcOps v2.4 Release Readiness

Review date: 2026-09-09. Status: release candidate deployed; final shadow audit,
activation record, and stable publication pending.

## Current Outcome

The public dashboard, bounded public A2S history, authenticated Reader portal,
and two guarded Operator workflows are implemented and production-verified in
signed candidate `v2.4.0-rc.9`. The independent availability exporter,
create-only off-host archive, read-only recovery, scheduled collection,
failure classification, and alert route are also proved.

The remaining release gate is the fresh Decision 22 shadow window from
`2026-09-09T15:00:00Z` through `2026-09-10T15:00:00Z`. Its terminal audit must
wait until `2026-09-10T15:05:00Z`, evaluate exactly 1,440 mature slots, preserve
every missing slot as bad, pass evidence-integrity checks, and meet the 99.5%
shadow target. Until then, `API-01` remains `Draft` and v2.4 remains a release
candidate.

## Claim Boundary

This record covers one single-node control plane, one controlled external game
server, and the deployed v2.4 Web experience. It does not claim high
availability, multi-region resilience, long-term reliability, an achieved SLO,
or real-player adoption.

Raw availability records, host and provider identifiers, credentials, operator
contact data, database payloads, and command payloads remain outside Git. Only
sanitized conclusions and public revision identifiers belong here.

## API-01 Release Boundary

Decision 23 defines `API-01` as at least 99.5% good primary minutes over seven
rolling days: 10,080 expected slots and at most 50 bad minutes. The shorter MVP
window gives earlier feedback but has more variance than the original 30-day
proposal and does not prove long-term reliability.

A successful 24-hour shadow audit permits the immutable activation tuple to be
recorded and starts the first prospective seven-day window. The shadow and v2.3
soak samples are not imported. Stable v2.4 publication requires measurement to
be activated, but it does not wait seven more days or claim the objective has
already been met. The first met-or-missed SLO review is a post-release result.

## Finalization Procedure

1. Keep the primary check, monitor revision, alert route, and production runtime
   unchanged through the recorded shadow boundary.
2. After the maturity grace period, run the read-only `Availability Shadow
   Audit` with `window_end_utc=2026-09-10T15:00:00Z`.
3. Require matching identity, 1,440 mature and evaluated slots, valid counts,
   and a met 99.5% shadow target. Do not remove or reinterpret missing slots.
4. If the audit passes, record the activation timestamp, owner, primary
   location, monitor revision, evaluator revision, report query, and alert
   route without importing shadow samples.
5. Update this document and the release notes with sanitized final evidence,
   run all repository gates, and publish stable `v2.4.0` from the verified
   candidate artifacts.
6. After seven complete prospective days, publish the first reproducible
   `API-01` result as met or missed without changing the target to fit it.
