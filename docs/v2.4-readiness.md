# GoldSrcOps v2.4 Release Readiness

Review date: 2026-09-11. Status: stable release published from the verified
candidate artifacts; `API-01` is collecting prospective evidence.

## Current Outcome

The public dashboard, bounded public A2S history, authenticated Reader portal,
and two guarded Operator workflows are implemented and production-verified in
signed stable release `v2.4.0`, promoted from candidate `v2.4.0-rc.9`. The
independent availability exporter,
create-only off-host archive, read-only recovery, scheduled collection,
failure classification, and alert route are also proved.

The final revised-policy shadow audit passed in
[run 34495816614](https://github.com/tov-vl/gold-src-ops/actions/runs/34495816614):
all 1,440 expected slots were mature, evaluated, and good, with no bad, missing,
pending, duplicate, or ignored non-canonical records. The immutable `API-01`
tuple started the prospective seven-day window at `2026-09-10T16:45:00Z`.

The Decision 22 shadow window from `2026-09-09T15:00:00Z` through
`2026-09-10T15:00:00Z` has passed its terminal audit. `API-01` moves from
`Draft` to `Collecting` only at the recorded activation boundary. Stable v2.4
publication completed after that boundary.

## Claim Boundary

This record covers one single-node control plane, one controlled external game
server, and the deployed v2.4 Web experience. It does not claim high
availability, multi-region resilience, long-term reliability, an achieved SLO,
or real-player adoption.

Raw availability records, host and provider identifiers, credentials, operator
contact data, database payloads, and command payloads remain outside Git. Only
sanitized conclusions and public revision identifiers belong here.

## API-01 Release Boundary

Decision 23 defines `API-01` as at least 99.5% good primary minutes over seven
rolling days: 10,080 expected slots and at most 50 bad minutes. The shorter MVP
window gives earlier feedback but has more variance than the original 30-day
proposal and does not prove long-term reliability.

The successful 24-hour shadow audit permits the recorded activation tuple to
start the first prospective seven-day window. The shadow and v2.3 soak samples
are not imported. Stable v2.4 publication does not wait seven more days or
claim the objective has already been met. The first met-or-missed SLO review is
a post-release result.

The complete activation tuple, including owner, primary location, monitor and
evaluator revisions, report query contract, alert route, and review boundary,
is recorded in [service-level objectives](service-level-objectives.md).

## Finalization Procedure

1. Completed: keep the primary check, monitor revision, alert route, and

   slots.

   verified candidate artifacts without rebuilding them.
6. After seven complete prospective days, publish the first reproducible
